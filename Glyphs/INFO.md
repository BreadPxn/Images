# Glyphs

Glyphs are symbolic emblems used to visually represent a character's identity without relying on text.

Every character has exactly three Glyphs:

- **Race Glyph** – Represents what the character is.
- **Personality Glyph** – Represents the character's dominant personality trait.
- **Activity Glyph** – Represents the character's primary occupation or role.

These three Glyphs together provide a quick visual summary of a character.

The three corresponding Glyphs communicate those traits even without reading the character profile.

Each Glyph category has its own design language while remaining visually consistent across the project.
